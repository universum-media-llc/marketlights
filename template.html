Colour Guide

Every colour on this page means one thing only.

Traffic Light

Green: favourable. Rising.
Amber: caution. Possible bottom or sideways; wait for confirmation.
Red: danger. Falling or overheated.
Grey dashed: not rated. Too volatile for a signal.

Trend & Chart Lines

Gold: uptrend. Price above its 50-day average. Also the positive sign.
Blue: downtrend. Price below its 50-day average. Also the negative sign.
Pink dotted: 50-day average (50d). Medium-term trend.
Grey dashed: 200-day average (200d). Long-term trend, the most watched line.

Price Levels

Green: support. A floor where buyers stepped in before. A close below it is a negative sign.
Red: resistance. A ceiling where sellers stepped in before. A close above it is a positive sign.
Purple: where price sits. Pale = near its 2-year low; dark = near its record.
Rose: distance from the record. How far price is below its all-time high.

RSI & Market Mood

Soft blue: oversold. RSI below 30: sold hard, often near a bounce.
Soft orange: overbought. RSI above 70: bought hard, often near a pullback.
Mood bar: red = extreme fear, green = extreme greed.
Dollar green: the US dollar box. Arrow up and "Strong" = a headwind for crypto and gold; arrow down and "Weak" = a tailwind.
